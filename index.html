ArmBrain Project

Shoulder Joint Development

This project is part of the broader ArmBrain robot arm system, with the J2 shoulder joint as the current focus of development. The repository documents the J2 design, mechanical build, testing, and ongoing work toward a full 6DOF arm with cycloidal reducers and a custom haptic glove for teleoperation.

Hardware

  • Motors: Nema 23 (J2) + Nema 17 (J1, J3–J5)
  • Reducers: Custom 3D printed cycloidal drives
  • Sensors: AS5600 magnetic encoders with diametric magnets
  • Files: Nema 23 Shoulder v7.step, nema 17 v13.step
